# 🚗 Insurance Claims & Policy Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze insurance policy sales, premium collections, and claim behavior across different vehicle and policy categories.

The dashboard provides a high-level view of insurance business performance and helps identify segments contributing most to policies, premiums, and claims.

The project focuses on visual storytelling, KPI monitoring, and trend analysis using Power BI.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem / Objective

Insurance companies need visibility into:

- Which vehicle categories generate the highest number of policies?
- Which segments contribute most to claims?
- How do premiums compare against claims paid?
- Which policy categories show higher claim exposure?
- How has insurance performance changed over time?

This dashboard was designed to simplify performance monitoring and support business decision-making through interactive analytics.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Motor-cycle policies account for the highest number of insurance policies opened
- Trucks and Pick-up vehicles also contribute significantly to total policy count
- Toyota is the leading vehicle manufacturer by policy volume
- Total Premium Collected ($383.9M) is higher than Total Claims Paid ($337.3M)
- Premium-to-Claim ratio stands at 113.8%, indicating overall positive premium coverage
- Average Premium ($7K) is slightly higher than Average Claim ($6K)
- Certain usage categories show significantly higher claim intensity compared to others
- Claims activity peaked during the 2015–2016 period before declining afterward
- Scatter plot analysis shows some low-premium policies generating disproportionately high claims
- A small number of policy groups contribute heavily to total claim payouts

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing vehicle segments such as Motor-cycles and Trucks
- Investigate policy categories with unusually high claims despite lower premium contributions
- Reassess pricing strategies for segments generating high average claims
- Monitor high-risk policy groups identified in scatter plot analysis
- Improve claim risk assessment for categories with elevated claim concentration
- Use yearly trend analysis to identify factors behind claim spikes during 2015–2016
- Strengthen profitability monitoring using premium-to-claim ratio tracking
